<h3>Display Name</h3>
<hr>
<p>Sometimes, your students may not know you by your default name shown on
SLS.</p>
<p>You may change the display name to an alias that your students identify
you by. This display name will be used when you leave Announcements or
Comments for students.</p>
<hr>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Open the right menu and click <strong>Edit Profile</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the Edit Profile page, under the Profile section, enter the name you
would like your students to see under <strong>Preferred Name</strong>, found
below the Avatar.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Save</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Default Email Address</h3>
<hr>
<p><strong>For Teachers in Schools</strong>
</p>
<p>The default email address used in SLS for teachers in schools is your
iCON email (ending with @<a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/customise/set-email-notifications/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">moe.edu.sg</a>).</p>
<p>If you do not have an iCON email, you will have your CES email as default
(ending with @<a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/customise/set-email-notifications/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">schools.gov.sg</a>).</p>
<p>If you have neither iCON nor CES email, your default email should be the
official email address in your MIMS account.</p>
<p><strong>For MOE HQ Officers</strong>
</p>
<p>The default email address used in SLS for teachers in MOE HQ is your WOG
email (ending with @<a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/customise/set-email-notifications/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">moe.gov.sg</a>).</p>
<h3>Setting an Alternate Email Address</h3>
<hr>
<p>Instead of using the default email address, you may also choose to provide
an alternate email address for SLS system notifications. <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/customise/set-email-notifications/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>SLS Notifications</u></a> and
verification codes will be sent to this email, if specified.</p>
<ol>
<li>
<p>From the homepage, open the right menu and click <strong>Edit Profile</strong>.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Set an Alternate Email Address" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/Cu-EditProfile.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>Under Account Settings, click <strong>Set</strong>  <strong>Email Address</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If you are logging in to SLS for the first time, you will be prompted
to enter a verification code. Check your email immediately to obtain the
verification code.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Enter your Alternate Email in the space provided.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Save</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>A confirmation prompt will appear. Click <strong>Proceed</strong> to send
the verification code.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Check your email immediately to obtain the verification code.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Enter the 6-digit verification code and click <strong>Proceed</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p><u>Note</u>: The verification code is only valid for 10 minutes.</p>
<h3>Set Email Notifications</h3>
<hr>
<p>You can choose to receive email notifications from SLS. Notifications
will be sent to your default email address or alternative email address
(if specified).</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>From the homepage, open the right menu and click <strong>Edit Profile</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Under Account Settings, click <strong>Set</strong>  <strong>Email Notifications</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select the checkboxes to the categories you wish to receive email notifications
from:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Class Group Announcement</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Assignment</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Forum</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Resources</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Admin (Announcements from all Content Maps, Modules, Media and Reports)</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Save</strong> to confirm.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Customise an Avatar</h3>
<hr>
<p>A graphical representation of a user, also known as an Avatar, is used
to represent you.</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Open the right menu and click <strong>Edit Profile</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the Profile page, click <strong>Customise Avatar</strong> under Profile.</p>

</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can either select 1 from the 24 preset avatars, or build your own.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To build your own Avatar, click <strong>Build My Own</strong>. To return
to the preset Avatars, click <strong>Choose Presets</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can select the base colour of your Avatar by clicking the colour palette</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>
<br>
</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: auto; width: 660.25px;" height="auto" width="100%" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/Cu-AvatarPalette.png">

<p>

</p>
</li>
<li>
