<h2>Set Assignments as Assessments</h2>
<hr>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>There are multiple ways to set assignments as Assessments.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon) on the
Assignment card and click <strong>Manage</strong>. Alternatively, click
on the Assignment card to open the Assignment, open the right menu and
select <strong>Manage Assignment Settings</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the Assignment Settings subpage, click the <strong>Others</strong> tab.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select the checkbox beside <strong>Set as Assessment</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p><u>Note</u>:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Assessment cards are differentiated with a beige background.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Set Assignments as Assessments" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/As-Assessment.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>Students will not be able to access embedded links or apps in the Assignment
unless they have been whitelisted for use in the Assessment's network.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Students will not be able to view Assessments on their Assignments tab
on the homepage or the Assignments list. You will have to provide them
with the Student Attempt URL for them to gain access to it by clicking
the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon) when inside an
Assignment/Assessment.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Set Assignments as Assessments" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/As-AssessmentURL.png">
</div>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<p><u>Did You Know</u>:</p>
<p>If you do not wish to create an Assessment (which will require the whitelisting
of embedded links or apps), you may wish to <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/assess/add-and-view-access-codes/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>set a Access Code (formerly known as Passcode) to an Assignment</u></a>.
Students will require the Access Code before they can attempt the Assignment.</p>
