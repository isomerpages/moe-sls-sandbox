<h2>Create Assignments</h2>
<hr>
<p>You can create an Assignment by assigning a Module to students in your
Class Groups.</p>
<p>You can turn your <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/assess/set-assignments-as-assessments/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Assignment into an Assessment</u></a>.
An Assessment card is differentiated with a beige background.</p>
<p><u>Note</u>: For a better user experience, Assignments with over 100 assignees
will need to be split into multiple assignments with a maximum of 100 students.</p>
<h3>Assign a Module</h3>
<hr>
<p><strong>Important</strong>: If Module Permissions to allow teachers to
copy/assign/publish and students to copy this module are not checked, it
will not be possible to assign the Module.</p>
<ol>
<li>
<p>From the homepage, open the left menu and click Resources, followed by <strong>My Drive</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Open</strong> under the Module Title.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the Module View page, click the <strong>Assign</strong> icon</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: inline; margin: auto; width: 1.5rem;" height="auto" width="100%" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/Icons/Assign.svg">
</div>
<p>found on the top right of the header.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the Assign Module subpage, you will see all the Class Groups that are
assigned to you. Class Groups that you have created can be found here too.
Select the Class Groups you would like to assign the Assignment to, then
click <strong>Assign</strong>.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Create Assignments" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/AS-CreateAssignment1.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>Alternatively, from a <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/organise/about-class-groups/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Class Group</u></a> page
under the Assignments tab, click <strong>Add Assignment</strong> to assign
a Module.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the Assignment Title subpage, you may edit the <strong>Assignment Title</strong> and <strong>Permission Settings</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You may also Pause the Assignment upon assigning by checking the relevant
checkbox.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Create Assignments" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/AS-CreateAssignment2.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>You may also adjust the Permission Settings. These permission settings
will not affect your current Module in your My Drive, but subsequent copies
of this Assignment.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Next</strong> to proceed to the Assignment Summary Page.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Assign</strong>, found on the top right of the <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/assign/manage-assignments/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Assignment Summary page</u></a> to
assign the Module.</p>
<p><u>Note</u>: For Module with multiple Sections, it may take some time
for the Assignment to appear on your Assignment List.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Quick Assign from Class Group</h3>
<hr>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>From a Class Group, click <strong>Quick Assign</strong> and select <strong>Activity</strong> or <strong>Quiz</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Once selected, a paused untitled Assignment will be created in the Class
Group.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You will be directed to the <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/author/add-new-activities-and-sections/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Module Editor page</u></a> of
the Assignment.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Quick Assign Activities &amp; Quizzes" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/AS-QuickAssign1.png">
</div>
<h3>Quick Assign from Planner Event</h3>
<hr>
<ol>
<li>
<p>Create a <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/plan/create-class-events/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>New Event</u></a> in
the Planner for a Class Group.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The new Event will appear in the Planner tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click the <strong>+</strong> button to Quick Assign an <strong>Activity</strong> or <strong>Quiz</strong>.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: auto; width: 825.328px;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Quick Assign Activities &amp; Quizzes" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/AS-QuickAssign2.png">
</div>
<p></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Alternatively, you can click on the Event to open the Event Details. Then
click <strong>Quick Assign</strong> and select <strong>Activity</strong> or <strong>Quiz</strong>.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Quick Assign Activities &amp; Quizzes" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/AS-QuickAssign3.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>Once selected, a paused, untitled Assignment will be created in the Class
Group and attached to the Event.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You will be directed to the <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/author/add-new-activities-and-sections/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Module Editor page</u></a> of
the Assignment.</p>
</li>
</ol>
