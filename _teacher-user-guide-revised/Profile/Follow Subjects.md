<h3>Access My Subjects</h3>
<hr>
<p>My Subjects allow you to see your Assigned and Followed subjects to
<a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/track-progress/access-learning-progress/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>track Learning Progress</u>
</a>and search for relevant resources. Teachers and students will be able
to search for related Resources (eg. Modules, Question Types and Media
Objects) easily with the pre-selected filters.</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Open the right menu and click <strong>My Subjects</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The page will contain both your Assigned Subjects and Followed Subjects.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can access <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/track-progress/access-learning-progress/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Learning Progress</u></a> or
<a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/discover/search-for-resources/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Search for Resources</u>
</a>directly from the Subject card.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To access the Learning Progress, click the <strong>Learning Progress</strong> icon</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: inline; margin: auto; width: 1.5rem;" height="auto" width="100%" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/Icons/LearningProgress.svg">
</div>
<p>found on the top right of each Subject card.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The Learning Progress icon will be disabled on a Followed Subject card
if you do not have a Class Group tagged to that Subject and Level.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To search for resources related to a particular Subject, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
and click <strong>Search for Resources</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Follow &amp; Unfollow Subjects</h3>
<hr>
<p>You may follow other subjects of interest beyond the ones assigned to
you.</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Open the right menu and click <strong>My Subjects</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select <strong>Follow More Subjects</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Use the dropdown to indicate the Subject(s) and Level(s) you would like
to follow. Click <strong>Apply</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Follow</strong> to follow the subjects of interest.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To Unfollow a Subject, on the Followed Subject card, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
and select <strong>Unfollow Subject</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
