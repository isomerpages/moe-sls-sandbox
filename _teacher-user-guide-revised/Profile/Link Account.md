<h3>Link Account</h3>
<hr>
<p>Users are able to associate their iCON 2.0 or Google accounts with SLS.</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Open the right menu and click <strong>Edit Profile</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the Profile page, click <strong>Manage Linked Account</strong> under
Profile.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Sign in with Google</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>In the window that pops up, enter the email and password of the account
you wish to connect to the SLS account.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select the checkboxes that allow "<a href="http://ufinity.com" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">ufinity.com</a>" to access Google Drive files.
Select <strong>Continue</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If you have successfully linked the iCON 2.0/Google Account to SLS, the
linked email address will be shown.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: auto; width: 825.328px;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Manage Linked Account" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/Cu-LinkedAccount1.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>To unlink your iCON 2.0/Google Account, click <strong>Unlink</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
