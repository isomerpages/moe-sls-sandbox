<h2>About MOE library</h2>
<hr>
<p>The MOE Library contains resources that are created and curated by the
Ministry of Education (MOE), and designed to complement student learning.
Students and Teachers have access to all the modules for all levels.</p>
<p><u>Note</u>: Due to certain licensing agreements, students may be unable
to load some images, audio files, videos and documents if they are not
allocating the relevant subject or are accessing the SLS outside Singapore.</p>
