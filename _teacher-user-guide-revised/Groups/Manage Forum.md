<h2>About the Forum</h2>
<hr>
<p>The <strong>Forum</strong> allows teachers and students to post Topics and
Polls for discussions outside of Assignments. To access the forum, click
the <strong>Forum</strong> tab from any <strong>Class Group</strong> page.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Teachers can choose to <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/collaborate/change-forum-settings/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>allow Observers and Students</u></a> to
<a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/collaborate/add-a-poll/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>create Polls</u>
</a>or <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/collaborate/post-and-comment-in-the-forum/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Posts</u></a> within
<a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/collaborate/add-a-topic/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Topics</u>
</a>. Owners of the Posts and teachers can <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/collaborate/change-forum-settings/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>control commenting permissions</u></a>.</p>
<h2>Add a Topic</h2>
<hr>
<h3>Create a Topic in Class Group Forum</h3>
<hr>
<p>From R19, teachers can create Topics in Class Group Forum to better categorise
forum posts.</p>
<ol>
<li>
<p>From a Class Group, click the <strong>Forum</strong> tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Create New Topic</strong> and enter the following fields:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Header</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Featured Image (optional)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Body</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Keyword Tags (optional)</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>You may also choose to notify the class group about the Topic, and grant
Observers and students the permission to create posts for the Topic by
selecting the appropriate checkboxes.</p>
<p></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Create</strong> to proceed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To view all the topics, click <strong>View All ()</strong>. The number
in parenthesis shows you the total number of topics you have created for
a particular class group.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Edit or Delete a Topic from Class Group Forum</h3>
<hr>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Only teachers can edit and/or delete a Topic card.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To edit a Topic, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
and select <strong>Edit</strong> to edit the Header, Feature Image, Body,
and Keyword Tags, enable notifications to the class group, or grant Observers
and students the permission to create posts. Click <strong>Save</strong> to
confirm.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To delete a Topic, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
and select <strong>Delete</strong>. Click <strong>OK</strong> in the pop-up
message to confirm.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h2>Add a Poll</h2>
<hr>
<h3>Create a Poll in Class Group Forum</h3>
<hr>
<ol>
<li>
<p>From a class group, click the <strong>Forum</strong> tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Create New Poll</strong> and complete the Question Body field.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Under Poll Type, you may select either <strong>Multiple-Choice</strong> or <strong>Slider</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If you have selected a Multiple-Choice Poll, complete the Rich-Text-enabled
option. You can add up to 26 options.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If you have selected a Slider Poll, you may create up to 7 plain text
options.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You may also choose to notify the class group about the Poll, and hide
Poll results from voters.</p>
<p></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Create</strong> to proceed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To view all the Polls, click <strong>View All ()</strong>. The number in
parenthesis shows you the total number of Polls created for a particular
class group.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Edit or Delete a Poll from Class Group Forum</h3>
<hr>
<ol>
<li>
<p>The owner of the Poll and teachers who are Owners/Co-teachers can edit
and/or delete a Poll.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To edit a Poll, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
and select <strong>Edit</strong> to edit the Question Body, Poll Type, Poll
Options, and Keyword Tags. You can also notify your class group about the
Poll and change viewing permissions to Poll results. Click <strong>Save</strong> to
confirm.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><u>Note</u>: If someone has already responded to the poll, you can only
add/edit keyword tags and select/unselect the checkbox “Hide poll results
from voters“.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To delete a Poll, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
and select <strong>Delete</strong>. Click <strong>OK</strong> in the pop-up
message to confirm.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h2>Change Forum Settings</h2>
<hr>
<h3>Topics Settings</h3>
<hr>
<p>Teachers are able to change the permission settings to allow or disallow
Observers and Students to create posts within a topic.</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>When creating a new topic, select the checkbox “Allow Observers and Students
to Create Posts” should you wish to grant them permission to do so.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Complete the fields within the Create Topic subpage and click <strong>Create</strong> to
proceed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can also change the permission settings for an existing topic but
clicking the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon) and selecting <strong>Edit</strong>.
Alternatively, click a topic card, followed by clicking the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
and selecting <strong>Edit.</strong>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select or deselect the checkbox “Allow Observers and Students to create
Posts”.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p></p>
<h3>Poll Settings</h3>
<hr>
<p>Teachers are able to change the permission settings to allow or disallow
Observers and students to create a poll.</p>
<ol>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Forum Settings</strong>, then select the checkbox “Allow
Observers and Students to create Poll”.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Save</strong> to confirm.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Comment Settings</h3>
<hr>
<p>The owners of posts and teachers who are Owners/Co-teachers are able to
change commenting permission by turning it off or on.</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Enter a topic by clicking the topic card.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>For newly-created posts, select or deselect the checkbox “Commenting is
enabled” to allow or disallow others to add comments.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Create</strong> to proceed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>For existing posts, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
and select <strong>Comment On</strong> or <strong>Comment Off</strong> to allow
or disallow others to add comments.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p><u>Note</u>: Teachers can delete all comments.</p>
<h3>Post and Comment in the Forum</h3>
<hr>
<p>Owners and Co-Teachers can create <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/collaborate/add-a-topic/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Topics</u></a> in
a Class Group Forum. Posts can be created within each Topic.</p>
<h3>Create a Post</h3>
<hr>
<ol>
<li>
<p>Enter a topic by selecting a topic card.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To start a new post, click <strong>Create New Post</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Posts can contain texts, mathematical and chemical formulae, drawings,
file uploads and audio recordings.</p>
<p><u>Note</u>: Audio recordings can be downloaded and saved when it is being
processed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select or deselect the checkbox “Commenting is enabled” to allow commenting.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can add keyword tags to improve the appearance of the post in search
results.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To notify the class group about a new post, select the checkbox “Notify
Class Group”.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Create</strong> to continue.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Edit or Delete a Post</h3>
<hr>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>The owner of the post, Owners and Co-Teachers can edit and/or delete a
post.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To edit a post, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
and select <strong>Edit</strong> to edit the message, keyword tags of a post,
enable commenting or notification to the class group. Click <strong>Save</strong> to
confirm your edits.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To delete a post, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
and select <strong>Delete</strong>. Click <strong>OK</strong> in the confirmation
prompt to confirm.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Comment on a Post</h3>
<hr>
<ol>
<li>
<p><a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/collaborate/change-forum-settings/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Commenting permissions</u></a> can
be toggled on/off on individual posts by teachers or owners of posts.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select a topic card, followed by a post that you wish to leave a comment
on.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: auto; width: 660.258px;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Post and Comment in the Forum" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/C-TopicCard1.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>From the View Post subpage, click <strong>Add</strong> to enter comments.</p>
<p><u>Note</u>: Comments can contain mathematical or chemical formulae, emoticons,
drawings, file uploads and audio recordings.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If you wish to notify students of the comment, select the&nbsp;<strong>Notify student(s)</strong> checkbox.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Post</strong> to proceed and <strong>X</strong> to close the
subpage.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To edit or delete a comment, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
and select either <strong>Edit</strong> or <strong>Delete</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
