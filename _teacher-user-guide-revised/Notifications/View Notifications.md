<h3>View Notifications</h3>
<hr>
<p>From the Notifications page or Notifications tab (accessed through Class
Groups), you can sort your Notifications according to <strong>For Me</strong> and <strong>Created By Me</strong>.</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Under the For Me tab, you can further sort your Notifications by All,
Unread and Flagged.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Under both the For Me and Created By Me tab, you can filter your listing
using the on <strong>Viewing</strong> and <strong>Type</strong> dropdown list.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Viewing:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>All</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>General</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pinned Class Groups - Notifications relating to specific Class Groups
that you have pinned</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>My Class Groups - Notifications relating to specific Class Groups</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Admin Roles - Notifications relating to your additional Administrative
Role within SLS</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>Type:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>All</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Announcements - Class Group and System Announcements</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Others - Resource Status, Transcoding, Others, Forum, Assignment, Resource,
Module, Media, Reports</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
<p></p>
<p><u>Note</u>: When viewing Notifications from a Class Group, you will not
be able to filter your listing based on Viewing as you will only be able
to see Notifications for that selected Class Group.</p>
<h3>View Notifications For Me</h3>
<hr>
<ol>
<li>
<p>There are 4 ways to access your Notifications:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>click the <strong>Bell</strong> icon</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: inline; margin: auto; width: 1.5rem;" height="auto" width="100%" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/Icons/Bell.svg">
</div>
<p>on the header, followed by <strong>View All Notifications</strong>,</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>click <strong>View All Announcements</strong> from the Announcements widget
on the homepage,</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>open the left menu and click <strong>Announcements</strong>,</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>enter a Class group and select the <strong>Notifications</strong> tab.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: auto; width: 793.328px;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="View Notifications" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/N-ClassGroupNotification.png">
</div>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the Notifications page, the default view will show Notifications under
the For Me tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can sort your Notifications according to <strong>All</strong>, <strong>Unread</strong> and <strong>Flagged</strong> by
clicking the respective tabs.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can further filter your listing from the <strong>Viewing</strong> and <strong>Type</strong> dropdown
list. Alternatively, click <strong>Filter</strong> to open the Filters subpage.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><u>Note</u>: When viewing Notifications from a Class Group, you will not
be able to filter your listing based on Viewing as you will only be able
to see Notifications for that selected Class Group.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Apply</strong> to proceed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>For Notifications that require your action, clicking that <strong>Notification card</strong> will
redirect to you the relevant page.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: auto; width: 825.328px;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="View Notifications" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/N-Notifications2.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>For Notifications that are for information, clicking that <strong>Notification card</strong> will
open the Notification Subpage. Click <strong>X</strong> or <strong>View All Announcements</strong> to
return to the Notifications page.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: auto; width: 825.328px;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="View Notifications" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/N-Notifications1.png">
</div>
</li>
</ol>
<p><u>Did You Know</u>:</p>
<p>You can view the twelve latest Notifications by clicking the <strong>Bell</strong> icon
on the header. You will be able to view, flag, and unflag a Notification
from the notification panel.</p>
<h3>View and Edit Notifications Created By Me</h3>
<hr>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>From the Notifications page or Notifications tab (accessed from a Class
Group), select the <strong>Created By Me</strong> tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can filter your listing from the <strong>Viewing</strong> and <strong>Type</strong> dropdown
list. Alternatively, click <strong>Filter</strong> to open the Filters subpage.
For Notifications accessed from a Class Group, you can only filter your
listing from the <strong>Type</strong> dropdown list.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To edit a Notification, click the <strong>Notification card</strong> to
enter the Announcements subpage, followed by clicking the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
and selecting <strong>Edit</strong>.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: auto; width: 825.328px;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="View Notifications" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/N-AnnouncementCard.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Save</strong> to confirm your changes.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Delete Notifications Created By Me</h3>
<hr>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>From the Notifications page or Notifications tab (accessed from a Class
Group), select the <strong>Created By Me</strong> tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can filter your listing from the <strong>Viewing</strong> and <strong>Type</strong> dropdown
list. Alternatively, click <strong>Filter</strong> to open the Filters subpage.
For Notifications accessed from a Class Group, you can only filter your
listing from the <strong>Type</strong> dropdown list.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To delete a Notification, click the <strong>Notification card</strong> to
enter the Announcements subpage, followed by clicking the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
and selecting <strong>Delete</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Ok</strong> in the confirmation prompt.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Turn On/Off Notifications</h3>
<hr>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>From the Notifications page or Notifications tab (accessed from a Class
Group), you can turn on <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/customise/set-email-notifications/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Email Notifications</u></a> for
a particular category of Notifications from Notification cards.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon) and followed
by <strong>Turn On/Off Notifications</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>When Notifications are turned on, you will be able to receive them in
the email you have indicated in SLS.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Flag a Notification</h3>
<hr>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>On the Notification card, click the <strong>Flag</strong> icon</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: inline; margin: auto; width: 1.5rem;" height="auto" width="100%" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/Icons/Flag24.svg">
</div>
<p>to flag a Notification. You can use it to flag important notifications
you wish to follow up on.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>A Flagged Notification will be indicated with a <strong>Red Flag</strong>
</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: inline; margin: auto; width: 1.5rem;" height="auto" width="100%" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/Icons/FlagFilled24.svg">
</div>
<p>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To unflag a Notification, click the <strong>Red Flag </strong>icon</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: inline; margin: auto; width: 1.5rem;" height="auto" width="100%" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/Icons/FlagFilled24.svg">
</div>
<p>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To flag/unflag on multiple Notifications, you can select multiple Notifications
by selecting the checkboxes beside the Notification cards, or selecting
the checkbox beside <strong>Select all notifications</strong> if you wish
to select the entire page of Notifications.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the table header, click <strong>Flag</strong>/<strong>Clear Flag</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p><u>Note</u>: You are unable to Flag Notifications that are created by
you.</p>
<p></p>
<h3>Mark a Notification as Read</h3>
<hr>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Unread Notifications are indicated with a yellow circle on the Notification
card.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: auto; width: 825.328px;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="View Notifications" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/N-Notifications2.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>The yellow circle on the Notification card will disappear when the card
is opened.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To mark multiple Notifications as read, you can select multiple Notifications
by selecting the checkboxes beside the Notification cards, or selecting
the checkbox beside <strong>Select all notifications</strong> if you wish
to select the entire page of Notifications.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the table header, click <strong>Mark as Read</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
