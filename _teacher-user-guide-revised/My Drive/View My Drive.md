<h2>Search in My Drive</h2>
<hr>
<h3>Search in My Modules</h3>
<hr>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Open the left menu from the header on the homepage. Click <strong>Resources</strong>,
followed by <strong>My Drive</strong>. By default, you will be in the My
Modules tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To search for the Resources under the My Modules tab, enter your search
term(s) in the <strong>Search My Modules</strong> search bar</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Press "Enter" to view your search results.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Results to the search term(s) will be listed. You can sort by Title, Type,
and Last Modified through the header.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>For Resources under My Modules tab, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
to <strong>Share</strong>, <strong>Make a Copy</strong>, <strong>Set as Template</strong>, <strong>Move</strong>, <strong>Move to Trash</strong> or <strong>View Details</strong>.
You can only share or make a copy of the Resource if the permission settings
have been enabled.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Search in Trash</h3>
<hr>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Open the left menu from the header on the homepage. Click <strong>Resources</strong>,
followed by <strong>My Drive</strong>. By default, you will be in the My
Modules tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click the <strong>Trash</strong> tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To search for Resources in Trash, enter your search term(s) in the <strong>Search Trash</strong> search
bar.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Press "Enter" to view your search results.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Results to the search term(s) will be listed. You can sort the Title,
Location, Type, and Deleted date through the header.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>For Trash, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
to Restore or Delete Forever.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>View Modules Shared with Me</h3>
<hr>
<p>Modules shared with you can be accessed from My Drive.</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Open the left menu from the header on the homepage. Click <strong>Resources</strong>,
followed by <strong>My Drive</strong>. By default, you will be in the My
Modules tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To view the Modules teachers have shared with you, click <strong>Shared With Me</strong>.
<br>
</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: auto; width: 825.328px;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="View Modules Shared with Me" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/O-SharedWithMe.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>To search for Modules shared with you, enter your search term(s) in the <strong>Search Shared With Me</strong> search
bar.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Press "Enter" to view your search results.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Results to the search term(s) will be listed. You can sort by Title, Type,
Last Modified and Owner through the header.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>For Modules in the Shared With Me page, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
to <strong>Make a Copy</strong> or <strong>View Details</strong>.
<br><u>Note</u>: You can only copy a Module if permission has been granted
by the Module Owner.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To return to My Drive, click <strong>My Drive</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Deleting a Shared Module</h3>
<hr>
<p><strong>Important</strong>: This only removes the module from your Shared
with Me page and does not remove the module from the teacher who shared
it with you.</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>To delete a shared Module, in the Shared with Me page, open the Module.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Open the right menu and select <strong>Share with Teachers</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Find your name in the Share with Teachers subpage and click <strong>Delete</strong>.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: auto; width: 825.328px;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="View Modules Shared with Me" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/O-DeleteShare.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Save</strong> to confirm the changes.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The shared Module will no longer be visible under your Shared with Me
page.</p>
</li>
</ol>
