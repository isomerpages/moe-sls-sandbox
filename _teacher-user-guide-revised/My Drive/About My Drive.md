<h3>Access My Drive</h3>
<hr>
<p><strong>My Drive</strong> can be accessed from the homepage by opening
the left menu.</p>
<ol>
<li>
<p>In My Drive, you will see three tabs:</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>My Modules</strong> - where Modules created or copied by you are
stored and organised.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>My Templates</strong> - where you can find your personal Module
Templates.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Trash</strong> - where deleted Resources are listed.</p>
</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can view Modules shared with you from the <strong>Shared With Me</strong> page.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>By default, you will be on <strong>My Modules</strong> tab.</p>
</li>
</ol>
