<h3>Generate Passcode for Students</h3>
<hr>
<p>Owners and Co-teachers can create a Passcode (formerly known as two-hour
temporary password) for students in their Class Groups. This Passcode grants
students temporary access to the SLS if they had forgotten their password
or if their accounts had been locked.</p>
<p>However, when students use a Passcode to log in, they will not be able
to access <strong>My Profile</strong>, and hence cannot change their password,
security question answers, or email address.</p>
<p><u>Note</u>: For a permanent solution, students can follow this guide
on <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/administer/reset-student-passwords-for-classes/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Student - Reset Password</u></a> to
reset their passwords. If they still encounter difficulties, and you are
unable to reset their password for them, you should contact the SLS Helpdesk
or your school's SLS Administrator. Only the SLS Helpdesk and SLS school
administrator are able to unlock a student's account. The SLS Helpdesk
will need to verify a student's identity using the security questions that
were set up when his/her account was first activated.</p>

<h3>Generate Passcode</h3>
<hr>
<ol>
<li>
<p>Select the Class Group containing the student(s) you want to generate
a Passcode for. Click the <strong>Admin</strong> tab to view Group Details.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Below Subgroup List, click <strong>Generate Temporary Passcode</strong> under
Passcode Settings.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Enter a Passcode and re-enter it for confirmation.</p>
<p><u>Note</u>: The Passcode must follow the password guidelines.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Generate Passcode</strong>.</p>
<p></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On the confirmation prompt, verify that the correct class group has been
selected.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Proceed</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p>Note:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>The Passcode needs to be entered at the existing SLS login page and not
the MIMS login page.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Students can either use their own password or the Passcode generated to
log in to SLS.</p>
</li>
</ul>
