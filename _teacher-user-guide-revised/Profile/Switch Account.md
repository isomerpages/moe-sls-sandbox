<h3>Switch to Student Account</h3>
<hr>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Teachers are able to use <strong>Student View</strong> to experience SLS
as a student. <strong>Teacher View</strong> and <strong>Student View</strong> are
not dependent on each other.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Open the right menu and click <strong>Switch to Student Account</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Alternatively, enter a Student Group or a Professional Development Group
and click <strong>Switch to Student Account</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can add your teacher-student account into a Class Group to attempt
Assignments as a student. To find out more on how you can add a teacher-student
account into a Class Group, click <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/organise/add-teachers-as-students-to-a-class-group/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>here</u></a>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
