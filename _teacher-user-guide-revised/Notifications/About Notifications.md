<h3>About Notifications</h3>
<hr>
<p>You can view Notifications sorted under <strong>For Me</strong> and <strong>Created By Me</strong> tabs.
Under the <strong>For Me</strong> tab, you can further sort your listing
by <strong>All, Unread</strong> and <strong>Flagged</strong>. You can also
filter your listing using the <strong>Viewing</strong> and <strong>Type</strong> dropdown
list.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Viewing: All, General, Pinned Class Groups (if applicable), My Class Groups,
Admin Roles (if applicable)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Type: All, Announcements (Class Group Announcement, System Announcement),
Others (Resource Status, Transcoding, Others, Forum, Assignment, Resource,
Module, Media, Reports)</p>
</li>
</ul>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>You will receive Notifications when:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>System, School and Class Group Announcements are created or updated.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Module resources are shared with you.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>a Discussion Topic or Poll has been posted in the Class Group Forum.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>status of a Module that has been sent to the Community Gallery is updated.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>status of a Media Object that is in the process of transcoding is updated.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>students’ marks and responses for an Assignment are ready to be downloaded.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>You will be able to Flag/Unflag a Notification and select Notifications
to Mark As Read.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Unread Notifications are indicated with a yellow circle on the Notification
cards and on the Bell icon</p>

</div>
<p>found on the header of the homepage.
<br>
<br>
</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: auto; width: 825.328px;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="About Notifications" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/N-New.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>Should you wish to receive SLS Notifications through your email, you will
have to <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/customise/set-email-notifications/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>enable email notifications</u></a>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
