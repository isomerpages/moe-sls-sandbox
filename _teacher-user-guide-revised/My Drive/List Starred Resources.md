<h2>Star Resources</h2>
<hr>
<p>Starred Resources allow both teachers and students to star up to 300 resources.
Teachers can star Modules, Questions, Media Objects and Apps. You may star
resources found in the MOE Library, Community Gallery, or My Drive that
you want to view or review at a later time.</p>
<p>When creating Modules, you can also <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/author/add-existing-from-library-and-starred-resources/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>add existing resources that you have starred</u></a> to
Modules.</p>
<p>Starred Resources can be found in the left menu, under <strong>Resources</strong>.</p>
<h3>Starring from Module View</h3>
<hr>
<ol>
<li>
<p>From the Module View page, you can star the following:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>a Module by clicking the <strong>Star</strong> icon beside the Module Title
in the banner</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>a Question by clicking the <strong>Star</strong> icon on the Component cards</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Media Objects by clicking the <strong>Star</strong> icon on the Media Object</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: auto; width: 793.328px;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Star Resources" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/O-StarMediaObject.png">
</div>
</li>
</ul>
<p><u>Note</u>: While Activities cannot be starred, Teachers can add Activities
from a starred Module. Find out more <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/teacher-user-guide/author/add-existing-from-library-and-starred-resources/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>here</u></a>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Starring from Search Results</h3>
<hr>
<p>You can star a Resource (Module, Question, or Media Object) by clicking
the Resource card, followed by the <strong>Star</strong> icon found at the
top of the subpage.</p>
<p></p>
<h3>Starring from My Drive folders</h3>
<hr>
<p>You can star a Module by clicking the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon)
followed by <strong>View Details</strong> of Modules located in the My Drive
folders. Alternatively, click <strong>Open</strong> to enter the Module View
page. Click the <strong>Star</strong> icon beside the Module Title in the
banner.</p>
<h2>Unstar a Resource</h2>
<hr>
<ol>
<li>
<p>Open the left menu and click <strong>Resources</strong>, followed by <strong>Starred Resources</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To unstar a Module, click <strong>Open</strong> under the Module title.
At the Module View page, click the yellow <strong>Star</strong> icon</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: inline; margin: auto; width: 1.5rem;" height="auto" width="100%" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/Icons/Unstar.svg">
</div>
<p>to unstar the Module.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><u>Note</u>: To unstar multiple resources, select the checkbox next to
the title of the resource before clicking <strong>Unstar</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To unstar a Question or Media Object, click <strong>View Details</strong>.
At the Details subpage, click the <strong>Star</strong> icon to unstar the
Question or Media Object.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Alternatively, if you are viewing a Starred Resource, click the <strong>Star</strong> icon
again to unstar it.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><u>Note</u>: The Star icon will no longer be lit yellow when you successfully
unstar the resource.</p>
</li>
</ol>
