<h2>Create Subgroups</h2>
<hr>
<h3>About Subgroups</h3>
<hr>
<p><strong>Important</strong>: Only class group Owners and Co-teachers are
able to create Subgroups.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Subgroups can be created within any class group.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The purpose of a Subgroup is to organise your students into smaller groups.
By organising your students this way, you are able to achieve the following:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>assign modules to specific Subgroups</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>grant access to sections/activities for specific Subgroups</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>assign Team Activities and gamified modules to specific Subgroups</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<h3>Create Subgroups</h3>
<hr>
<ol>
<li>
<p>From the Class Group page, select a <strong>Class Group</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Under the <strong>Admin</strong> tab, click <strong>New Subgroup</strong>.</p>
<p></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Enter the Subgroup Name.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Add Students</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can add students to the subgroup by searching for their names.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can also filter and sort students based on the following fields:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Form Class</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Index No.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Name</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Level</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>School (useful for class groups that contain students from more than one
school)</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can hide students from all subgroups by clicking the <strong>Settings</strong> icon</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="box-sizing: inherit; font-family: Lato, sans-serif; max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: inline; margin: auto; width: 1.5rem;" height="auto" width="100%" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/Icons/Settings24.svg">
</div>
<p>and selecting the checkbox beside <strong>Hide students from all subgroups</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Check the students to be added to the class group and click <strong>Add</strong>.</p>
<p></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Complete the subgroup creation by clicking <strong>Save</strong>, followed
by <strong>X</strong>. The newly-created subgroup will appear in the form
of a card under the Subgroup List.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p><u>Note</u>:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Subgroups without students can be created.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Subgroups with the same name as other subgroups can be created.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Subgroup cards are arranged by alphabetical order; you are advised to
name subgroups with suitable prefixes to better organise them.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Each student can be added to more than one subgroup.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3>View Subgroups</h3>
<hr>
<ol>
<li>
<p>From the Class Group page, select a <strong>Class Group</strong>, followed
by the <strong>Admin</strong> Tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Under the Subgroup List, you can view the list of subgroups that have
been created.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="View Subgroups" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/DI-ViewSubgroups1.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click the individual <strong>Subgroup</strong> card to view the details
of each subgroup.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Edit a Subgroup</h3>
<hr>
<ol>
<li>
<p>From the Class Group page, select a <strong>Class Group</strong>, followed
by the <strong>Admin</strong> Tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Under the Subgroup List, you can view the list of subgroups that have
been created.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click on the <strong>Subgroup</strong> card that you wish to edit.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You may edit the following:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>the Subgroup Name</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>add or remove students</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Edit &amp; Delete a Subgroup" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/DI-EditSubgroups1.png">
</div>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Save</strong> to confirm your changes, followed by <strong>X</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>Delete a Subgroup</h3>
<hr>
<ol>
<li>
<p>To delete a Subgroup, click the <strong>Overflow</strong> (<strong>3-dot</strong> icon).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>Delete</strong>.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Edit &amp; Delete a Subgroup" src="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/images/2Teacher/DI-EditSubgroups2.png">
</div>
</li>
<li>
<p>A confirmation prompt will appear to confirm your action.</p>
<p><u>Note</u>: Action cannot be undone.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click <strong>OK</strong> to confirm.</p>
</li>
</ol>
