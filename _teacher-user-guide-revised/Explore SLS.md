<h3>Navigating the User Guide</h3>
<ol>
<li>
<p>The left-hand menu serves as a holistic view of the various functionalities
available in SLS.</p>
<p><u>Note:</u> The list is not exhaustive and some pages might be nested
within certain categories.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>SLS functionalities have been divided into 3 broad categories - Tools,
Locations and Resource components.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To navigate to the pages you need, you may expand the tool selected to
view its subpages by clicking on the dropdown button or click on the tool,
location or resource component itself.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Each tool, location or resource component subpage has been organised according
to the intended user flow [give more details].</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p>For more information on Tools, Locations and Resource Elements, click
on the hyperlinked pages.</p>
